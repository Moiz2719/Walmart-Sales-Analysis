# Project overview

## Business question

Which customer and subscription characteristics are associated with churn in the supplied customer database, and how can churn analysis help a business prioritise retention work?

## Objectives

- Combine customer, subscription, and support tables using `customerid`.
- Standardise selected fields and date columns.
- Define churn from the presence of a cancellation date.
- Calculate churn and retention rates, segment churn, average monthly charges per customer, tenure, monthly charges at risk, and support-escalation metrics.
- Create chart outputs for monthly churn, plan-level churn, state-level churn, and variable relationships.
- Turn results into clear, cautious business questions rather than causal claims.

## Data described by the notebook PDF

The notebook shows 21 customer records, 21 subscription records, and 9 support records. The database was referenced as `/content/customer_churn.db`; its file was not included with the supplied PDF.

## Expected deliverables

- Reproducible cleaning and feature-engineering scripts.
- Reusable SQL queries.
- KPI and segment tables.
- Visualisations saved as PNGs.
- Documentation of fields, definitions, and reported notebook outputs.
